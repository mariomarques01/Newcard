# Elemental VFX

`elemental-novas.webp` is a shared 2 by 2 atlas generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP at quality 90 with alpha preserved. The live canvas crops each quarter using the actual loaded dimensions. It supplies fire explosion, electric shield impact, arcane vortex and golden holy nova textures. Combined with character performances, source charge, directional projectiles, shock waves and deterministic particles.

Generation prompt: premium fantasy card battle game VFX atlas, exact regular 2 by 2 grid, transparent outer background, no borders, text, characters, UI or grid lines. Top left circular orange fire explosion with rolling flames, molten core, wispy smoke and sparks. Top right icy cyan electric shield impact with luminous hexagonal energy barrier, branching electricity and shattered blue crystal. Bottom left violet arcane vortex with dimensional portal, lightning filaments, purple smoke and pink white core. Bottom right golden holy nova with sunburst rays, gold dust, ornate magical ring and star sparks. High quality hand painted game VFX sprites, emissive cores, translucent feathered smoke edges, screen/additive blend, centered effects contained in their own equal cells.
